Personal Pronouns

"YOU"
Tú

INICIO I He She It You(singular) We You(plural) They

¿Que es?

"You" se usa para hablar con VARIAS personas a la vez. 2, 3, 10, todo el salón.

What is?

"You" (plural) is the second-person plural pronoun. It refers to multiple people you are talking to.

Ejemplos / Examples

You love music. = Tú amas la música.

You have a dog. = Tú tienes un perro.

You need rest. = Tú necesitas descansar.

You work hard. = Tú trabajas duro.

You want coffee. = Tú quieres café.

You live far = Tú vives lejos.

You speak clearly. = Tú hablas claro.

You eat fast. = Tú comes rápido.

You understand everything. = Tú entiendes todo.

You play well. = Tú juegas bien.
